Check numbers

Booklets are registered by their leaf range; a typed number is accepted with or without the prefix. This is how the no-duplicate rule and the bank matching know the same leaf.

Statement import

A bank without its own reader uses "Generic columns": an Excel or CSV whose headings say date, check number, description, debit, credit and balance (the running balance is checked on import).
