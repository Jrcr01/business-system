## 2026-09-28
- **Bank reconciliation you can finish in the app** - on Bank and cash → Reconciliation, each bank line that needs a look can now be dealt with right there: link it to one of your checks (for a typo in the number), open the check to correct it, or tag why it is fine (bank error, legacy record, customer bounced check, expected bank debit) with a note. Made a mistake? Reopen it. Each imported statement shows **Open** until every line is dealt with, then **Reconciled**.
- **Smarter matching** - bank debit memos are matched to your bank-loan installments (same amount, within 5 days of the due date) and bills payments to your credit-card statements (within 10 days). When a cleared check is one digit off from yours, or has two digits swapped, or matches an unnumbered check of the same amount dated within a week, the app **suggests** the match - you accept or reject it; nothing is linked by itself. Checks cleared before the system started are set aside automatically.
- **Reconciliation statement (new tab, with PDF)** - the bank balance, less your checks the bank has not cleared yet (listed), equals the cash really available; plus the bank items that are not your checks and how each was resolved, and a sign-off line.
- **Bank and cash on the Dashboard** - a new panel shows the bank balance and its date, how many of your checks the bank has confirmed cleared, the checks suppliers have not deposited yet, bank lines still needing a look and the lowest cash balance expected in the next 30 days, with the same alerts as Payment run. "Stale checks" no longer counts a check the bank statement shows as cleared.
- **Due today counts as still owed** - the Dashboard's "Total AP outstanding" and the combined outstanding summary now count a check dated today and a bank-loan installment due today as still owed (the money has not left yet), the same as the Schedule of payments.
- **Bank and cash in the overall report** - the Overview report (screen and PDF) has a Bank and cash section for the period: bank balance at the end, money in against the previous period, checks the bank cleared, checks not yet cleared, bounced customer checks and lines still needing a look. When no statement covers the period it says so. The PDF's contents page now shows the right page numbers.
- **Reports are easier to read and export** - every report table uses plain column names (Check #, Check date, Payee instead of check_number, creditor_name) with money as ₱1,234.56 and dates as Sep 28, 2026. Every download now offers **Excel** next to CSV (the Excel file has a sheet per table, money and dates formatted, ready to add up). Reports open faster when you switch filters.
- **Schedule of payments filters** - besides track and month, pick any date range and one or more payees; the tables, CSV / Excel and PDF all follow the filter. The Overview report now shows its trend chart and check activity on screen too, and names with "&" (like S&P) no longer break PDFs.
- **Bank alerts on Payment run and Bank and cash** - cash forecast dropping under your buffer in the next 7 days, a large bank debit nobody has explained, checks not cleared 7 days after their date (change the days under Reconciliation → Alert settings), and a bank statement more than a week old.

## 2026-09-27
- **Cash insights on Bank and cash** - worked out on this laptop from your bank statements and what you owe (nothing is sent anywhere): a day-by-day cash forecast with your own low-cash line, the checks suppliers have not deposited yet (by how long they have waited), money in by week and by type with slow weeks marked, unusual items (bounced customer checks, bank debits that are not your checks, unusually large lines), and working capital (bank cash against what is due in 7, 30, 60 and 90 days). Import a recent statement for real figures - the page warns when the latest bank balance is old.
- **Bank and cash (new page)** - download your Statement of Account from Metrobank MBOS and drop the file in. The app reads every line, checks that the running balances add up, and matches your checks by number and amount, so each check shows the date the bank really cleared it. You only look at the exceptions: an amount that differs, a cancelled check the bank still cleared, a customer check that bounced, or a check the system does not know. It also lists the checks suppliers have not deposited yet. The same file is never imported twice, and overlapping months are fine. Payment run reminds you when the last statement is more than a week old.
- **New look and a menu on the left** - the 10 tabs across the top are now a menu in the left sidebar, grouped as Daily work, Other payables, Records and reports, and System. The sidebar is Slick's blue, every page has the same heading style, statuses are coloured (Draft grey, Parked orange, Issued blue, Paid green, Cancelled red), money shows as ₱1,234,567.89 in every table, and each screen's main button (Park, Save, Create, Generate) is the filled blue one. The logo is no longer on screen (printed statements and reports still have it). Alt+1 ... Alt+0 now open the menu pages in order; hover a menu item to see its key.
- **Payment run (the new first page)** - at the top, today's quick report: what's due today (on a weekend, what's due on Monday), checks dated today, what's due in the next 7 days (the same figure as the Dashboard) and anything overdue and not covered, with a one-page "Print today's report" PDF. Below it, the payment cycle step by step: batches that still need checks, parked checks waiting for Chrysanth, runs to mark as printed (you can mark them right there), printed checks that need numbers (opens that batch - still one batch at a time) and statements to print. The number beside "Payment run" in the menu is how many things are waiting.
- **Add data** - one page for typing in new records: invoices and credit memos (pick the supplier, then the batch - its open Draft batch is suggested, or a new one), credit card statements, loan notes and installments, bank loans and their schedules, new creditors, checkbook booklets and spoiled checks. These are the same forms as on their own pages, with the same checks and confirmations.
- **Order statements, tidier** - the open batch shows its status in colour, the money (net, checks, balance) and a progress line: invoices, checks parked, sent to Chrysanth, numbered. The Checks part has four buttons instead of one long page: Issue checks, Checks in this batch, Assign numbers, Corrections. Promote statement status and the Vendor SOA matcher moved to "More tools" at the bottom.
- **Saved messages you can read** - after saving, a small "Saved ..." note appears in the corner; before, the page refresh wiped the message before you could read it.
- **Screens respond faster** - the app used to redo all 10 tabs every time you clicked or saved anything, even though you only see one. Now it only works on the tab you have open: switching tabs or clicking takes about a quarter to half a second instead of 2 to 3 seconds, and a save refreshes in well under a second. Nothing about how you use it has changed.
- **Program history backed up off Google Drive** - the nightly backup now also saves the program's complete version history as one file in C:\AP_Data\code_backups (the laptop's own disk), in case Google Drive's syncing ever damages it.
- **Library versions locked** - requirements.txt (and the full list in requirements-lock.txt) now pin the exact versions the app is tested with, so an upgrade can't break it by surprise.
- **Monthly restore test** - double-click restore_test.bat: it rebuilds the newest Google Drive backup on a temporary copy, checks it, starts the app on it and says PASSED or FAILED. Nothing live is touched.
- **Only this laptop can open the app** - other devices on the same Wi-Fi can no longer reach it (there is no password, so this closes that gap). Open it as before at http://localhost:8501.
- **Full credit card numbers removed** - the app only needs a card's last 4 digits; the full numbers are removed from the database, old backups and copies (remove_card_numbers.bat).
- **Bulk statements: include checks not yet printed - your choice** - a new switch in Print statements in bulk. On (as before) lists every check. Off leaves out checks that are not printed yet, so the statements, the invoice status and the summary page show only printed checks. A check counts as printed once its Chrysanth run is marked printed (Batch run history > Mark as printed); parked checks and checks in a run not marked printed yet (like RUN 005) are left out.
- **Summary page in bulk statement printing** - the combined PDF now starts with a cover page listing every included order statement (supplier, status, invoices, net total, checks and their total, with grand totals), followed by each statement. The same table shows on screen before you generate; a switch turns the cover page off.
- **No more near-empty pages in the batch statement** - a statement that overflowed a page by only a row or two used to leave a page holding one row (or just the footer), for each copy. The rows are now tightened just enough to fit on one page when that saves a page, and a leftover space at the end of a copy no longer spills onto a page of its own (bulk statement printing included).
- **No more stranded headings on the batch statement** - a section heading (like Checks) is no longer left alone at the bottom of a page with its table on the next one; if there is not room for the heading plus the first rows of its table, the heading moves to the next page together with the table.
- **Clearer invoice status on the batch statement** - the printed statement no longer says 'Unpaid' for an invoice whose checks are already issued. It now reads Unpaid (no check yet), Partly covered, 'Check issued . 2026-12-29' (fully covered by checks not yet cleared; the date is the last check) or Paid (all its checks cleared). The statement's own status at the top (Draft / Parked / Issued) follows the same rule as the browser. Only the printed labels changed - payables and reports are unaffected.
- **Order statement status: Draft / Parked / Issued** - the status shown in the Order statement browser (and its filter) now reads: Draft = no checks yet, Parked = checks are parked but none printed yet, Issued = at least one of its checks has already been exported / printed, Cancelled.
- **Print statements in bulk** - in the Order statement browser, Print statements in bulk makes one combined PDF for many order statements at once: limit it to Since last check print (created or changed on or after the day checks were last printed), Today, Last 7 days or All, untick any you don't want, and pick Batch Statement (A4) or Thermal 80mm. Nothing is locked or marked printed, so you can reprint after a revision.
- **Assign a whole order statement's check numbers in sequence** - in an order statement's (or loan note's) Checks tab, under Assign a check number, a new section 'Assign all N unnumbered checks of this batch in sequence' lines up the booklet's next free leaves against that batch's checks (leaves already used or spoiled are skipped). Review the list, then one confirmation assigns them all for that batch - all or nothing, every leaf re-checked when saving.
- **Search everything** - press / (or the Search everything button in the sidebar) and a search pop-up opens with the cursor ready: type a check number, invoice number, batch / loan reference, a name, TIN or contact, a card's last 4 digits or an exact amount - the list below filters as you type - and click a result to jump straight to it. It looks across checks, invoices, order statements, loan notes, bank loans, creditors, credit cards and spoiled checks.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** - Enter says Yes and Esc goes back in confirmation pop-ups; Alt+1 ... Alt+0 switch the main tabs; / jumps to the search box; Alt+P presses the screen's main Park / Save button; Alt+N opens the New Batch / New Loan Note / New Bank Loan form. The full list is in the sidebar under Keyboard shortcuts.
- **Error checks show in the booklet audit** - the gap audit (Admin & audit) now marks leaves you logged as spoiled as 'Spoiled (error check)' with their reason, counts them in a new Spoiled metric, and no longer shows them as open stock or missing gaps. A number that is both spoiled and issued is flagged as critical.
- **No duplicate check number - a hard rule in the database** - a check number can now exist only once, as one check or one spoiled entry, however it is typed (2554301 and 3992554301 are the same leaf). The database itself refuses a duplicate, so no screen, script or import can get around it. (Needs the database update: run update_database.bat; first run remove_duplicate_spoiled_checks.py --apply to clear the one duplicate spoiled entry, 3992554303, otherwise that part is skipped.)
- **Spoiled checks are validated** - Log a spoiled check now refuses a number that is already logged as spoiled or already belongs to a check on file, stores it in the full 10-digit form (7-digit typings count as the same leaf), and asks you to confirm if the number is not inside any registered booklet's range.
- **Mark a Chrysanth run as printed** - Chrysanth never remembers what it has printed, so the app now does. Batch run history shows Printed / Not printed yet for every run, with a Mark as printed button (and Un-mark) for the run you pick, and the Checks tab warns you about runs that were exported but are not marked printed yet. (Needs the database update: run update_database.bat.)
- **Chrysanth file name shows the order statements** - the downloaded file is now named with its run number and the order statements it holds, e.g. ChequeTransaction_RUN004_OS0006-OS0012.txt (a single one shows OS0006; lender checks add _Lenders), the same in Batch run history.
- **Chrysanth prints in order-statement order** - the stand-in cheque number of an unnumbered check now starts with its order statement number (then the check), so Chrysanth's Batch Print Order 'Cheque No Ascending' prints OS 0001, then OS 0002, and so on; lender checks come after. Checks exported earlier keep the numbers Chrysanth already has. (Needs the database update: run update_database.bat.)
- **Run number on every Chrysanth cheque** - the Remark of every cheque in an export file is now just its run number (RUN 004), also shown in the Batch run history. In Chrysanth's Cheque Transaction History type the run number in the Remark box at the top and Search, so only that run is loaded before you select and print (the filter arrow on the Remark column only hides rows - its header tick-box still selects them).
- **Last modified column and filter** - the Order statement browser, the Loan notes list and the Bank loans list now show when each record was last touched (the record itself, its invoices, checks, memos or repayment rows, or any logged change) and can be filtered by it: Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, All time or a custom range.
- **No check number = not final** - a check that has no check number yet can now always be edited (date and amount) or un-parked, even if it was already exported to Chrysanth (the screen reminds you to change it there too). Once a check has its number it is final and goes through Correct an active check, Cancel or Replace.
- **Typed checks are never lost** - checks you type into an order statement's Issue a check grid are now remembered even if you forget to park them. Open another order statement, come back, or refresh the page and they are still there (your split choice too). A yellow reminder says they are typed but not parked yet, the sidebar lists every batch that has some, and the browser asks before you close the page. They stay until you park them. (Needs the database update: run update_database.bat once.)

## 2026-09-26
- **Un-park checks** - in a batch's Checks tab there is now an "Un-park checks" section: pick parked checks (no number yet, never printed or exported) and take them back. After a confirmation they are deleted completely with their invoice links, the balance is available to park again, and if the batch is left with no active checks it goes back to Draft and keeps its OS number (it will not get a new one when you park again). Numbered, printed or exported checks are never touched - cancel or replace those.
- **Park checks now confirms, matches invoices and promotes the batch** - clicking Park Metrobank checks first shows a confirmation listing every check with the invoice it pays and for how much (a tick when it matches the invoice, a warning when it only pays part). After you confirm, the checks are parked, tied to their invoices, and a Draft batch is promoted to Issued automatically with its permanent OS number, shown in the confirmation message.
- **Mini calendar fixed and easier to move around** - clicking its arrows no longer sends you back to the Dashboard tab; you stay on the tab you were working in. It also has a month picker and a year picker now, besides the arrows, and it shows the same grey holiday / marked days as the Dashboard's payment calendar (hover a grey day to see why). Picking a month looks forward: in September, choosing January jumps to January of next year (the year picker can still change it). The Dashboard's payment calendar does the same.
- **Issue one check per invoice** - in a batch's Checks tab there is now a "How to split" choice: By number of checks (as before) or One check per invoice. The second makes one row per invoice still to pay, dated on that invoice's due date and for its remaining amount; dates and amounts stay editable, and each check is tied to its own invoice automatically.
- **Due dates now follow the bank's rule** - terms of whole months (30, 60, 90, 120, 150 days) land on the same day of the month as the invoice, not on an exact day count: an invoice dated Sep 8 on 120 days is due Jan 8 (it used to be Jan 6). A month with no such day uses its last day. COD and other terms (like 140) still count days. Invoices already saved keep their due date until they are saved again.
- **Chrysanth paid-version file** - Checks & checkbook now makes the file as a plain MS-DOS-style .txt file (the same format as Chrysanth's own ChequeTransactionTemplate.txt that imports successfully - amounts without .00) (AccountNo, Payee, Amount, Date, ChequeNo, CrossingOption, Remark, Place), ready for Chrysanth's File > Import Cheque Transaction, instead of the CSV. First save your Chrysanth account number and crossing under "Chrysanth settings". Chrysanth needs a cheque number on every row, so a check with no number yet gets a temporary placeholder number (starting with 9) in the file only - nothing is assigned in the system, and you assign the real numbers after printing. The batch history can re-download the file for any earlier batch.

The Edit / delete a statement list follows the same filter. Turn the switch off to see everything.

## 2026-09-21

- **Create schedule from existing checks** - on a loan note whose checks aren't tied to its repayment schedule, a new section gives each check its own schedule row (date = check date, amount = principal, interest 0) after you confirm. Nothing is saved if the checks add up to more than the loan's principal.
- **Correct a printed loan note check** - under a loan note's Checks tab there is now a "Correct a numbered check (number, date or amount)" section. Change the check number, date or amount right in the table and Save; every change is logged. It warns you if the change no longer matches the repayment schedule.
- **Retract a wrongly assigned check number** - in Assign a check number there is now an "Assigned the wrong number? Retract it" section. It takes the number off the check and, if it came from a booklet, puts the leaf back with no false gap.
- **Assigning a check number is safer** - a number you type directly now always wins over the booklet dropdown (it used to be ignored), you get a confirmation showing exactly which number will be used, and the typed box clears after each assignment.
- **Chrysanth CSV export** - the checks-to-print export is now a CSV in Chrysanth's own layout (Date, Account No, Payee, Amount, Cheque No, Remark, A/C Payee Only). Every check is marked A/C Payee Only = Y, Account No is left empty, and Cheque No stays empty until a number is assigned. Fixed: the download button used to disappear the moment the batch was logged; it now stays, and every past batch in Batch run history has its own Download CSV button.
- **Credit card payments come from the next statement** - you record statements only; the balance carried over on the next statement tells the system what was paid on the earlier one. The finance charge field is available on every statement.
- **Finance charge on every statement** - the simple entry mode now also has an optional field for the finance charge shown on the statement, so card interest feeds the cost-of-financing figures.
- **Credit card payable no longer double counted** - a balance carried over from one statement into the next is now counted once. Your current numbers do not change.
- **Smarter invoice-number matching** - SI-00123, SI 123 and 123 are recognised as the same invoice number, and a pop-up warns when you add one that matches an invoice already on file.
- **Possible duplicates report** - Reports > Possible duplicates scans every supplier and batch (migrated ones included) for the same number written differently, the same amount on the same date, and batches with the same total. Tick a finding and mark it as not a duplicate to stop it showing.
- **Startup check** - if the program has been updated but the database has not, the system now says so in plain words and points to the new Update Database file (update_database.bat), instead of showing a confusing error.
- **Credit card payment confirmed by the next statement** - when you add a statement you can leave the amount paid as unknown (ticked by default). It stays counted as owed and is marked 'Not yet confirmed'. When the next statement arrives, type the balance it shows as carried over and the system works out how much was paid on the earlier one and updates its status.
- **Schedule of payments sorting** - within each day the schedule now lists Lender, Bank Loan, Supplier, then Credit Card, and the smallest amount first inside each.
- **Simple credit card statement entry** - when adding a statement there is now a switch, "I only have the total amount due and the due date". Turn it on and you type just the total amount due and the due date (and any amount paid); previous balance, new charges and interest are left blank.
- **Overview report** - Reports > "Overview - highlights & insights": pick Day, Week, Month, Quarter or Year and see highlights written in plain sentences, paid vs scheduled, where the money went by track, top payees, cost of financing, payment behavior, what is coming up next period and a watch list, all compared with the previous period. Short and full versions, both with a PDF. The PDF now has a cover page (logo, period, three headline numbers, contents) and bar charts - trend over the last 6 periods, paid vs scheduled by track, top payees, coming up by week and interest by source (PDF only; the screen stays as tables).
- **Loan-note check warning quieter** - migrated loan notes that have no repayment schedule on file are no longer flagged for having checks not tied to a schedule row.
- **Bank names in full** - banks appear as BDO Unibank, Inc., Bank of the Philippine Islands, Philippine National Bank and so on everywhere; typing an abbreviation like BDO still finds the right bank.
- **Creditor names in proper case** - supplier, lender and bank names read in proper case (BDO Unibank, Inc., CSS Autoparts Inc.). Names you type in ALL CAPS or all lowercase are tidied automatically; a name typed in mixed case is left as you wrote it.
- **Dates shown in your local time** - created dates and times (batches, creditors, audit log, print times) now show in local time, so the "Today" filter includes what you created after midnight.
- **Excel invoice-entry form** - a macro-enabled Excel workbook with a pop-up form (supplier list, batch tab, Doc Ref, date, amount, Less %) that writes each invoice to the right supplier tab, warns on a repeated Doc Ref, and produces exactly the workbook the multi-batch import reads. Files are in the excel_invoice_entry folder. The workbook can now be downloaded from the system (Order Statement > New Batch > import section), with the supplier list refreshed at the moment of download.
- **Import many batches from one Excel file** - on the Order Statement tab (New Batch), upload one workbook where each sheet is one batch of invoices, named after its supplier. The system matches each tab to a supplier, shows you a review, then creates a new batch per tab, imports its invoices and (optionally) promotes it to Issued. The same supplier can have several tabs.
- **Promote right after import** - when you add invoices to a draft batch from a spreadsheet or photo, a checkbox (ticked by default) also promotes the batch to Issued in the same step.
- **Shorter draft numbers and named templates** - new draft batches are numbered like DRAFT-26-09-01 (year, month, then a running number that restarts each month) instead of a long string of digits, and the invoice CSV template downloads as SupplierName_DraftNumber_invoice_template.csv. Existing drafts keep their old numbers.
- **Double-recording reminder** - before saving, a pop-up asks whether you still want to continue if something looks already recorded. Most important: supplier invoices and checks, loan-note checks, bank loan monthly payables, and credit card statements (same card, due date and amount due). Also new loan notes, bank loans, cards, suppliers and credit memos.
- **Loan note checks must match the schedule** - each check has to equal its installment's principal + interest on its due date. A warning shows on the loan note if any check doesn't, and a pop-up asks before you save an edit that breaks the match.
- **Cancel bank loan** - a bank loan with a schedule can be cancelled: it stays on record but stops counting as an amount due.
- **New reports** - batch rundown and invoice rundown (paid / partially paid / pending, with running balance and PDF), plus PDFs for the three master rollups. PDFs now fit their text at 9pt.
- **Supplier terms** - 140 and 150 days added; new suppliers default to 120 days.
