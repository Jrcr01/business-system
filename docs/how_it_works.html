How the system works: follow one tire

From the day you order it to the day the money is counted. Seven steps, most of them a minute or two.

1

shopping_cartYou order it

Laptopa few minutes

Pick the supplier and the tires. The calendar shows which pay day is light. The supplier confirms, and it becomes a purchase order.

8 Sunny 11R22.5 are coming, and roughly when the check falls due.
2

move_to_inboxIt arrives

Phone · Stock in1 minute

At the gate, the order is already on the screen. Type how many came and the DR number. Press Received.

8 tires are in stock, and you owe the supplier for them on a set date.
3

labelYou label it

Laptop + printeronce per delivery

On the order, "Print labels" gives one sticker per tire. Stick them on. The sticker is what the phone reads from now on.

nothing new. This step makes every later step a scan, not typing.
4

outboxA customer buys two

Phone · Stock out30 seconds

Write the invoice by hand, as always. Scan the two tires as they leave. Press Done.

6 are left. Two went out at 11:05, and their invoice is still to be recorded.
5

receipt_longYou record the invoice

Phone · Record a saleor laptopat the counter, idle, or at night

Type the invoice number and the amount you received, and how it was paid. A trade-in or surplus tire is added here with its estimated cost.

the sale, the profit on it, and whether the customer still owes.
6

fact_checkYou pay what is due

Laptop · Daily payablesmornings, 5 minutes

The list shows what falls due today: supplier checks, loan payments, cards. Tick what the bank has paid. The bank statement confirms it later.

what is paid, what is still owed, and which days ahead are heavy.
7

summarizeMonth end

Laptop · Income statementnothing to type

The system adds it up from everything recorded: sales, less what those tires cost, less expenses.

whether the month made money, and where it went.

What you can ask it at any moment

How many do we have?Scan or type a size on the phone. On hand, oldest DOT, last in, last out.
What do we owe, and when?Suppliers, lenders, bank loans and cards, day by day, with the heavy days marked.
Did we make money?Per sale, per day, per month. Counted from the cost of what was actually sold.
You do not have to start everything at once. Each step works without the ones after it.
  1. Now: pay from Daily payables, and record the day's invoices in the evening. No labels needed.
  2. Next: order through a purchase order and receive on the phone, so stock and payables fill themselves in.
  3. Then: count the stock brand by brand and label it. After that, selling is a scan.
Two icons on the phone: Slick's ERP is the whole system; Slick's POS is the five buttons for the shop floor.The invoice is always written by hand first. The system records it; it does not replace it.
