Phone pages when the signal drops mock-up · suggestions 1 and 2

Two changes to the phone pages that already exist (Stock count, Stock in, Stock out, Record a sale, Take in). Nothing about how a count or a delivery is done changes. 1: a save that went through but whose answer was lost can never be recorded twice. 2: what has been scanned is kept on the phone itself until it is sent, so closing the app, a refresh, a call or a flat battery loses nothing.

What does not change

  • No working for hours offline: a new count, a new search or a new page still needs signal (that is suggestion 3, after the warehouse test).
  • The laptop pages: as now. Money pages already save all-or-nothing.
  • Kept scans live only on that phone, for that person. Signing out of the phone asks first if any are waiting. They are cleared after 7 days if never sent.
