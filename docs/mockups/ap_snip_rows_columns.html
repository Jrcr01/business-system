Mock-up: fix the rows and columns of a snip yourself (like DataSnipper)

Owner, 2026-10-17: "I think it is the row and column. Can you do like the DataSnipper, I can fix the rows and columns?" and "make it sizable like DataSnipper" (the box). On Price lists, Bank loans and the SOA matcher, after a table box is drawn, the system shows where it thinks every column and row is, as purple lines on the picture. You drag, add or remove lines, then press Read: each cell is read on its own, inside the lines you set. Sample: your Ultra Plus batteries photo.
