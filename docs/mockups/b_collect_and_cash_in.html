Stage B mock-ups: collect, deposit, and see cash in by day

The buying side answers "what goes out, and when". Stage B answers the other half: what comes in, and when — collections on charge sales, customer checks to deposit, and a cash-in line beside every day's cash-out. Your rules drawn in: oldest invoice first, partial payments fine, a PDC counts when it clears, the bank statement is the truth, everything flags and nothing blocks. Six screens; nothing is built.

What goes on the Review list

  • A bounced check (its invoice owed again)
  • An invoice overdue past the customer's terms; an unusually high balance
  • A write-off (admin only, reason kept) and any recovery after it

What does not change

  • Recording sales stays exactly as it is now — Stage B only adds what happens after a charge sale.
  • The daily limit keeps working as today; "room = in − out" is a switch, off until you turn it.
  • The bank import, the AP side, the Review list: untouched.
  • New tables (collections, customer checks, write-offs), migration at release — sandbox first, your click-through, then one 12 AM–6 AM release.
