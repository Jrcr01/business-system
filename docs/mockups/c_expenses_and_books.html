Expenses & petty cash — and the first look of the Books it feeds

Your go (2026-10-11): real books — GL codes, debits and credits, the five journals, subsidiary ledgers, Trial Balance — built as a layer that derives every entry from the documents the family already types. Expenses come first, so the books are honest from day one. Four screens; nothing is built.

1 · Expenses — the drawer's money out

Anything paid from the drawer or the bank that is not a supplier payable: electricity, rent, meals, helpers' wages, shop supplies. One line each, a category from your own list, who took it and why. This is what makes the deposit differences and the day's profit true.

What stays true

  • The family's screens do not change — nobody at the counter sees a debit, ever.
  • The chart (~30 accounts, PH style) is yours to rename; expense categories become expense accounts automatically.
  • Hidden behind its own switch until your go, like Stage B. Sandbox first, backfill tested against live's copy: AR, AP and stock values must equal their controls.
