draw Stage A mock-ups: Sell, and the stock moves

Drawings only, with made-up figures. Nothing here is built. Read each screen, then answer the blue questions. I build only after your go.

What Stage A covers
  • Stock: the stock list, an item's history, counts you approve, labels and scanning, lending to and borrowing from the relatives' businesses.
  • Sell: sales invoice booklets, the counter sale on the PC and on the phone, goods released before the invoice, voids and returns, the day sheet.
  • The rule in every screen: the system flags, it does not block. A sale is never refused.
  • Not in Stage A: collections, customer checks, statements, cash in by day (Stage B); quotations, fleet prices, the customer page (Stage C).
