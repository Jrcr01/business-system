Phone-only features mock-up · six suggestions, nothing built

Each one uses something only a phone has: its Home Screen icon, its camera, its touch screen or its share sheet. Pick the ones you want; each would be built and released on its own.

1Shortcuts on the app icon

Long-press the Slick's icon on the Home Screen: straight to the job, without Home in between. Android (and the installed app on iPhone shows nothing — Apple does not allow it for web apps).

📞Phone
✉Viber
S'Slick's POS
📥 Stock in
📤 Stock out
🧮 Stock count
🧾 Record a sale
S'

Hold the icon, tap Stock in: the phone opens right on the order list. For the staff phone, Stock count and Stock in/out only.

What it takes: four lines in the app's settings file. The phone picks them up the next time the app is opened. Cost: very small.

📷 Take a photo of the receipt opens the camera; the picture is made smaller (about 200 KB) before it is sent.

Kept: unlike the snip, these pictures are stored (they are proof): beside the database on the server, in the nightly backup and the laptop's copy. About 6 MB a month at 30 receipts. Cost: medium. Also possible on the laptop by choosing a file.

3The collector signs on the phone when checks are released

At Release checks, the supplier's collector signs with a finger on the phone. The signature goes on the release slip (and its PDF) with the name, time and the checks released.

10:15▮▮▮
‹ Release checks
Slip RS-0118 · Tire Asia Group
3 checks · ₱1,056,432.00
Mark Santos
sign here · clear
Released: signed

Turn the phone sideways for more room to sign. The slip prints with the signature; the paper signature can stay too.

Kept: the signature as a small picture with the release (a few KB). Cost: medium. Releasing stays possible without signing (flag, don't block): an unsigned release shows "not signed".

6Scan a tire, see its price and stock

From Home, one button: the camera opens, point it at the tire's barcode, and the item shows with its stock, oldest DOT and the price list's price.

What it takes: the scanning and the lookups exist (Stock, Price list); this joins them behind one button. Cost: small.
