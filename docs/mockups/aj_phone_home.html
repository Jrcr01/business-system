Greyed: the tile is there, dashed and grey with "laptop"; its figure still shows (e.g. how many booklets are in use). Tapping it says "done on the laptop".

Hidden or greyed
  • Hidden: the cleanest phone Home; the family never sees what they cannot do there. Someone looking for Settings on the phone will not find it — it is on the laptop.
  • Greyed: you still see those pages exist and their figures; a few more tiles to scroll past, and taps that lead nowhere useful.
  • The menu (☰) follows the same choice.
To decide: hidden (left) or greyed (right)? My suggestion: hidden — it is what you asked for, and the grey tiles mostly add scrolling.
