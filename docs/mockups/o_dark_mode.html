Each person picks their own, and it follows them to every device they sign in on. "Auto" follows the phone or laptop's own light/dark setting. Everyone starts on Light.

Questions before I build
  1. The look itself: is this dark right, or should it be darker (near black) or softer (more grey-blue)?
  2. Headings in yellow on dark, as drawn, or keep them light blue?
  3. The switch: Light / Dark / Auto as drawn, or only Light / Dark?
  4. The sign-in page is already dark navy. I would leave it as it is in both modes.

What it involves: about 185 colours in the style files plus those written inside individual pages each get a dark partner, then every page is opened once in dark to catch any white box or unreadable text. Printing is untouched.
