3Every other stock movement, under FIFO

WhenWhat happens to the batches
Goods received (Receive, phone Stock in)A new batch: the date received, the DR, the pieces, the order's net price each.
The supplier's invoice has another priceThat delivery's batch takes the invoice price (the actual price prevails). Pieces of it already sold: their sales take the difference too, so their margin is right.
A delivery undone (recorded by mistake)Its batch is removed. If some of it was already sold, those pieces move to the next batch, and the sale's cost follows.
Customer return, voidThe pieces go back at the cost they left at, as a batch placed first, so they sell next.
Lent to / borrowed from an affiliateLending takes from the oldest batch like a sale; what comes back returns at that cost, placed first.
Count finds fewerThe missing pieces leave from the oldest batch (shrinkage at that cost).
Count finds moreA batch for the extra pieces at the newest batch's cost (the last price paid).
Sold before it was received (stock below zero)Still never refused, still on the Review list. See question 1.

5To decide

1. A tire sold before its delivery is recorded (stock reads below zero).
A) Recommended: the sale takes the last price paid for now; when the delivery is recorded, the sale is corrected to that delivery's cost. The margin ends up exact.
B) The sale keeps the last price paid; nothing is corrected later. Simpler, a little off.
2. The cost shown on the Items list
A) Recommended: "Next sale costs" (the oldest batch). B) The average of what is left. Both are on the item card, as in screen 1.
