Snip from a document mock-up: price lists, supplier SOAs, bank amortisation tables

Like DataSnipper, inside the ERP: the supplier's document on the left, the form on the right. Drag a box over a table (or a single figure) and it lands in the form, with the page and box kept so the figure can always be traced back. A PDF with real text (the price lists) gives exact rows; a scan or photo (most SOAs) is read by the server's OCR for just the box you drew, and the rows come up for checking first. Everything runs on your own server.
