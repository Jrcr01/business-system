Sample figures, not your data. An invoice is recorded on its own, the moment it arrives: it is a payable from then on. A payment statement is made only when you pay: you tick the invoices you are paying and make the checks. One invoice makes it a single payment, several a batch payment. Nothing already recorded is renumbered or moved.

Only the heading changes: "BATCH / ORDER STATEMENT" becomes "PAYMENT STATEMENT", with "Single payment" or "Batch payment" under it. The layout stays as you have it.

What stays the same
  • A delivery received on a purchase order still records its invoice by itself; it simply shows up on the Invoices page.
  • Inside an open payment statement you can still add an invoice directly, as today.
  • Checks, the daily limit, credit memos, "Many from Excel", "Print many" and every existing statement work as now.
Questions before I build
  1. Several suppliers at once: should Make checks accept ticks across suppliers and make one payment statement per supplier, or stay one supplier at a time as drawn?
  2. The supplier's printed copy: "PAYMENT STATEMENT" as drawn, or another heading for what the supplier receives?
  3. The draft's name: "Draft payment" on screen until it has its PS number: fine?
  4. Who may record an invoice here: everyone who can open Payment statements, or also the people who receive goods?
