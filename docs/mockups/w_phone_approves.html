What protects it

  • The number. Only the person looking at that computer can pick the right one of three. A wrong pick cancels the code.
  • The fingerprint. The phone must sign in to the system first, with the fingerprint already turned on for that phone.
  • The same person. The phone's account must be the email Cloudflare verified on that computer. Nobody can approve a computer for someone else.
  • One use, two minutes. A photo of the QR is useless afterwards.
  • Recorded. The sign-in record shows "Signed in: approved by the phone", and "It is not me" goes to Checks and review.

Who

  • Everyone except the Scanner account.
  • A person who has not turned on fingerprint on a phone does not see the link.

Later, if you want it stricter

  • One switch in Settings → Sign-in can make the phone a must on computers, with the password refused there.
