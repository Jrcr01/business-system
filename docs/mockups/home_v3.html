How it works
No separate slip: the statement is printed 3 times, headed SUPPLIER COPY, FILING COPY and FINANCE COPY. The end of each copy gets the receipt text and signature lines.

  1. Print the slip with the bundle (invoices + checks + statement).
  2. The supplier signs and dates. Supplier copy: the supplier keeps it. Filing copy: your mother keeps it, filed with the invoices. Finance copy: goes to finance for their records.
  3. She opens Checks → For release, picks the slip no., types who received and the date. All checks on the slip are marked released.
  4. You see, from anywhere: Printed, not collected or Released (date, received by).
