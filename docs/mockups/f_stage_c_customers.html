Sample figures, not your data. Stage C is four small things on top of what is already live: a quotation that becomes a sale, prices agreed with a customer, a follow-up list, and one page that tells a customer's whole story. Nothing here blocks a sale.

1 · Sell → Quotations

The same idea as Request → PO on the buying side: a quotation is written, sent, and when the customer says yes it becomes the sale, so nothing is typed twice. A quotation moves no stock and is not money owed.

Questions before I build
  1. Quotations: do you give written quotations today, and should the system number them (QT 0001…) and print one for the customer?
  2. "Good until": is 7 days the usual, or another number?
  3. Agreed prices: do you have customers with a standing price or discount today? If none, this screen can wait.
  4. Follow-ups: are the four reasons right (waiting quotation, overdue invoices, a regular who stopped buying, your own reminder)? Any to drop?
  5. Customer types: is "fleet / dealer / walk-in" the right set?
