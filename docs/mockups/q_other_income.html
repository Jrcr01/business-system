1. Bank → Other income (a new page, beside Expenses)

Money that comes in and is not a sale. It works the same way as Expenses, so there is nothing new to learn: one line each, a kind from your own list, where it was received, and from whom. A wrong line is taken back with a reason; nothing is deleted.
