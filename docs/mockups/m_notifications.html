Questions before I build
  1. Payment due: on the due date only ("due today"), or also a few days before? A payment made at a bank counter the same day is fine; an online transfer from another bank can take a day.
  2. Statement coming: on the statement day itself, or a day or two after, when the bank has usually sent it?
  3. Annual fee: one month before is my suggestion, so there is time to ask for a waiver. Is the fee amount worth keeping on the card too, or is the month enough?
  4. Who receives them: you for every card and each cardholder for their own, as drawn? Or you only?
  5. Time: 7:00 AM?
