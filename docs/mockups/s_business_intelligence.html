Business intelligence on Analytics every figure below is a made-up example, in the shape the real pages would have

Six tabs, following the BI ladder on SAP's page: what happened → why → what will happen → what to do, plus alerts and questions in plain words. The last two tabs are the decision support system (compare scenarios, highlight risks, guide next steps). Tick which tabs are necessary; the rest can wait.

1 · SalesWhat happened and why: sales, margin, by brand, size, customer, payment.
2 · StockWhat is tied up: value, fast and slow movers, days of stock, DOT age.
3 · CashWhat will happen: the bank balance 60 days ahead.
4 · WarningsRisks surfaced for you, each with a why and a link.
5 · DecisionsCompare scenarios: pay plan, reorder, price change.
6 · AskQuestions in plain words, answered from the system's own data.

How it works: the question goes to Claude (Anthropic's model) with a description of the system's tables; it writes one read-only query, the system runs it and draws the answer. It can only read, never change anything, and every question and its query are logged. Needs an Anthropic API key (about ₱0.50–2 per question). System admin only to start. This is the only place AI is used; price lists stay as they are.

What to tick

Each tab can be built on its own. My suggestion for the order: Sales + Stock (the charts fill in as sales are recorded), then Cash + Warnings, then Decisions, and Ask last once you add the key. Not in this mock-up on purpose: receivables and customers (Stage C), budgeting (parked), and anything that writes data from Analytics — the tabs only read.
