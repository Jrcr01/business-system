2The rules behind it

WhatHow
Cost on each lineThe cost the next sale of that brand takes (FIFO: its oldest batch). A tire with no cost yet shows "no cost" and must be typed.
MarkupPesos on top of cost, per tire. Default: at cost. Presets +100 and +200; "other" takes any amount. Picking a preset re-prices every line except the ones typed by hand.
RoundingPrices round up to the next ₱50 (2,627 → 2,650). A setting: none / ₱10 / ₱50.
PiecesOne number for the quote (default 4). A brand with fewer on hand is quoted for what is there, and says so.
WhoThe family (it shows costs). The staff phone has no Quote button.
Saved?No. A walk-in who canvasses and leaves leaves nothing behind. A sale started from it is recorded the usual way.

3To decide

1. Presets: A) At cost, +100, +200 and "other" (as drawn). B) add +300 / +500 too.
2. Round the price up to ₱50: A) yes (as drawn). B) no, cost + markup exactly.
3. Pieces: A) one number for the whole quote, default 4 (as drawn). B) per brand.
4. The bottom line on the customer view ("Mounting and balancing included? Ask us."): A) keep, editable in Settings. B) none.
5. "Start a sale from this": A) include it. B) leave it out for now.
