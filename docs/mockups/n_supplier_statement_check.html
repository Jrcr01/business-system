Sample figures, not your data. Statements come as photos and paper, in a different layout from each supplier, but every line is a document number, a date and an amount. So nothing is uploaded or read by the system. Instead the page lists what your records say you owe that supplier, and you go down the paper ticking what is on it. Only the lines that are on their paper and not in your records are typed.

What I assumed, and the questions still open
  1. No upload, no reading of photos. You tick and type from the paper. Right for "mostly photos and paper"?
  2. The amount compared is the one you recorded for the DR (after any discount). For a statement like Express Dymans, you would type their "Total amount" column, not the DR amount before the 40%.
  3. The supplier's number is matched against both your DR no. and your SI no., since some suppliers list invoices and others delivery receipts.
  4. RPG Battery and RPG Distribution: one supplier in your system, or two? If two, each is checked on its own.
  5. Who uses it: everyone who can open Order statements, or only you?
